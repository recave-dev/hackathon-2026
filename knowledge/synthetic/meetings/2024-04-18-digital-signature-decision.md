# Leadership product review — digital signatures

## Evidence and assumptions

Sales reported repeated interest in signing documents inside CivicFlow. The team did not yet have 25 named purchase commitments. Karol's model used 25 paid municipal customers within twelve months as a target, not as a current count.

## Options discussed

The group considered building a native feature, using a vendor, or deferring. Piotr said a native feature would offer better workflow control. The team did not record a measured outcome for the unchosen options.

## Decision

Marta approved an internal digital-signature feature. Lena owns delivery. The success measure is 25 paid municipal customers by 18 April 2025, using the billing feature report.

## Actions

Tomasz will collect named demand evidence during rollout. Karol will compare the target with paid uptake after twelve months.
