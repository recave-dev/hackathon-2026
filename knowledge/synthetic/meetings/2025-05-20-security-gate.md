# Limited pilot security and contract gate

## Reviewed evidence

ConnectorCo supplied an architecture diagram, retention schedule, named-account access plan, incident contact, and a draft exit export. Aneta tested the scope against the limited pilot proposed on 12 May. The team has not reviewed a broad production rollout.

## Gate decision

Aneta approved customer-data access for the **limited pilot only**, with named accounts, logging, and a documented termination export procedure. Lena may continue the partner pilot. Any wider rollout requires a new review if scope or data flow changes.

## Actions and unknowns

Piotr will verify the adapter in a test tenant. Adam will supply the final support handoff procedure before launch. Lena will confirm customers individually; passing this gate does not turn interest into paid activations.
