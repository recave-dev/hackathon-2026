# Leadership decision — CivicFlow e-Doręczenia

## Evidence and reports

Four named customers asked in writing for an integrated workflow. Tomasz reported twelve interested municipalities in Slack, but the full list and buying intent remain unverified. The prior digital-signature feature reached 14 paid customers against a target of 25, reminding the team to test adoption assumptions. Piotr estimated a 16-week internal build with two engineers already assigned to CivicFlow 4.2. ConnectorCo proposed a six-week path from kickoff, subject to review.

## Options and model

The team compared internal build, ConnectorCo partnership, and postponement. Karol's first-year scenario estimates were 140,000 PLN contribution for build at 18 paid customers and 198,000 PLN for partner at 22. At 14 paid partner customers, its estimate drops to 86,000 PLN. These figures are assumptions, not observed revenue or guaranteed outcomes.

## Decision

Marta approved **a paid partner pilot with ConnectorCo**, conditional on a security and contract gate by 20 May. She did not approve a full rollout. The rationale was a faster customer offer and a way to test demand without diverting the two CivicFlow 4.2 engineers. Lena owns the pilot. No decision was made that an internal build would never happen.

## KPI and review

Target: **10 paid municipal activations by 12 November 2025**, six months after this decision. Paid activation means an activated, billed customer account; expressions of interest and free testing do not count. Karol will use the billing export for the actual. Leadership will review adoption, support burden, and vendor terms at that date.

## Actions

Aneta: finish the security and exit review by 20 May. Piotr: define limited integration acceptance criteria. Tomasz: validate named paid-pilot candidates. Lena: own kickoff and maintain the decision record.
