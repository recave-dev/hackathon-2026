# Six-month partner pilot outcome

## Measured KPI

Karol's billing export shows **six paid municipal activations as of 12 November 2025**. The decision record set a target of ten, so the pilot is four below target. This is an observed six-month activation count, not the first-year contribution or a full-year uptake number.

## Interpretation and limits

The offer launched and six customers paid. Tomasz agrees that the twelve-account interest report from May was too broad to use as a paid-customer forecast. Ewa found that customers with a named workflow owner onboarded faster, while others needed more support and training. The meeting does not establish that an internal build or postponement would have produced a better result; those alternatives were not run.

## Lesson

For a future integration investment, require named customer commitments and an onboarding plan before relying on a broad interest count. Revisit the partner model with actual support effort and vendor terms rather than reusing the 22-customer first-year assumption unchanged.

## Next action

Marta asks Lena to prepare a new decision for 19 November: retain or renegotiate the partner arrangement, build internally, or pause expansion. No new option is approved today.
