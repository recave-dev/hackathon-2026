# Draft option model for the 12 May decision

Karol uses first-year estimated contribution = forecast paid customers × contribution per paid customer − fixed first-year cost. Every input below is a planning assumption, not an observed sale or guaranteed vendor term.

| Option | Time from kickoff | Forecast paid in first 12 months | Contribution per paid customer | Fixed first-year cost | Estimated contribution |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Build internally | 16 weeks | 18 | 20,000 PLN | 220,000 PLN | 140,000 PLN |
| Partner with ConnectorCo | 6 weeks | 22 | 14,000 PLN | 110,000 PLN | 198,000 PLN |
| Postpone | No release | 0 | 0 PLN | 0 PLN | 0 PLN from this offer |

The partner estimate changes to 86,000 PLN if only 14 customers pay in the first year. The 22-customer partner forecast is a scenario, not validated demand. The build case assumes two dedicated engineers and would compete with CivicFlow 4.2 work through 30 June. Security review, API compatibility, support ownership, and partner exit terms remain unresolved as of this draft.
