# Digital-signature feature: one-year review

On 18 April 2024, leadership chose to build a digital-signature feature internally. The approved case forecast 25 paid municipal customers within twelve months. The billing report on 18 April 2025 shows 14 paid customers for that feature.

The forecast missed by 11 customers. The feature is still used by those 14 customers; this review does not assign a causal reason for the gap. Sales had counted verbal interest more broadly than named purchase commitments. For future features, Tomasz will separate written requests, trial users, and signed orders before converting interest into an uptake forecast.

Lena's lesson for the e-Doręczenia question: test willingness to pay with a limited offer before diverting a full engineering team. This prior outcome is evidence about Aster's forecasting process, not proof that an internal e-Doręczenia build would fail.
